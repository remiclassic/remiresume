My game

Designed and developed by me, in production now.

MY GAME · DESIGNER + DEVELOPER · COMING TO STEAM

Norman Ascension.
One life ends. Your House continues.

Normandy, 1066. An online multiplayer medieval life and dynasty RPG set in a persistent shared world. You begin as an unknown eighteen-year-old with modest means and a name few remember. Work, trade, serve, break the law or practise your faith; marry, raise an heir, and carry your House into the next generation.

I am the game’s designer and developer: the life and dynasty systems, travel between Norman towns, markets, crafting and contracts, player chat, letters, factions and marriages between player Houses, asynchronous caravan interceptions, succession, and the House Chronicle that records each family’s story.

Game design, UX / UI & development · Strategic Sloth LLC · In development
The film and screenshots are unretouched in-engine captures of the game world: day and night, weather and the four seasons.
