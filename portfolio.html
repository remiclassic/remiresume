MY GAME · DESIGNER + DEVELOPER · LIVE ON GOOGLE PLAY

Tropic Tumble.
Merge stickers. Fill the island.

A free-to-play mobile merge puzzle. Drag a sticker onto its twin: two jelly stickers make a painted one, two painted ones make a shiny one, and two shiny ones are yours to keep. Every sticker collected has a place in a painted island scene, and a finished scene levels you up and opens a chest.

I designed and built all of it: the rules and physics, 40 levels across eight islands, 76 stickers in 14 sets, the economy, events, stories and a season pass, the interface, the music and the code. It is one hand-written file with no engine, wrapped for Android with ads and in-app purchases.

Game design, UX / UI & development · Strategic Sloth LLC · Released 2026

MY GAME · DESIGNER + DEVELOPER · COMING TO STEAM

Norman Ascension.
One life ends. Your House continues.

Normandy, 1066. An online multiplayer medieval life and dynasty RPG set in a persistent shared world. You begin as an unknown eighteen-year-old with modest means and a name few remember. Work, trade, serve, break the law or practise your faith; marry, raise an heir, and carry your House into the next generation.

I am the game’s designer and developer: the life and dynasty systems, travel between Norman towns, markets, crafting and contracts, player chat, letters, factions and marriages between player Houses, asynchronous caravan interceptions, succession, and the House Chronicle that records each family’s story.

MY GAME · DESIGNER + DEVELOPER · IN TESTING ON GOOGLE PLAY

Runefjord.
One game, a second world.

Tropic Tumble rebuilt as a Viking game. The rules, 40 levels, economy and code are the same; the 76 stickers, scene paintings, map, menus, music, words and store identity are new. It is a separate Android app with its own ads, in-app products and age rating.

It shows what a reskin of a finished game takes: four days from a copy to a signed build on a Google Play test track, with the game playable the whole time while art replaced stand-ins one picture at a time.

MY GAME · DESIGNER + DEVELOPER · PLAYABLE BROWSER DEMO

Sloth Jam.
A cozy party game for the whole family.

A 3D party game for kids aged six and up and the grown-ups next to them. A family of sloths lives on a small island with 27 one-minute party games. Between rounds you fish, garden, dress up and decorate your own plot. One stick, one button, and no reading needed: pictures and numbers only.

I designed and built all of it: the rules for every game, computer players at three levels, the island town, seven adventure courses, the wardrobe, the interface, the sound and the code. It is written in TypeScript on three.js and runs in a browser on a phone or a computer, with no account and no download.
