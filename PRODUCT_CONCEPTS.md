# Product concept studies — 2026

Current explores a mobile crypto wallet and desktop exchange. Signal Academy explores a gamified cybersecurity learning platform. These are new independent portfolio concepts informed by experience, not recovered client artifacts. Names, accounts, prices, dates inside the mockups, and learning records are sample content.

## Deliverables

- `portfolio-imgs/products/wallet.png` — three mobile purchase journey screens.
- `portfolio-imgs/products/exchange.png` — desktop exchange and expired quote state.
- `portfolio-imgs/products/academy.png` — learning dashboard and progression.
- `portfolio-imgs/products/lab.png` — practice lab and submitted assessment feedback.

Each study includes a four-step UX walkthrough, recovery paths, and proposed validation. No research outcomes or shipped implementations are claimed. The mockups are static artwork; UX flow navigation is interactive.

## Next implementation checks

Financial actions need validated amounts, explicit currency, server-side quote expiry, duplicate-order protection, pending status, and accessible feedback. Cyber labs need keyboard-accessible link inspection, inert examples, meaningful prerequisites, saved progress, and feedback that distinguishes suspicious indicators from definitive proof. Mockup sample chart values and progress graphics are illustrative rather than tested data visualizations.
