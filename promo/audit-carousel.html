7things that work

  1. 01
    Balanced, symmetrical layoutSecondary buttons sit at the edges. The action button stays front and centre.
  2. 02
    Easy flowThe main icons stay along the bottom on every screen. Nothing jumps around.
  3. 03
    Hierarchy through scale and colourHeader, player info, chests and the action button each read at their own level.
  4. 04
    Buttons where players expect themClose and Back keep the positions players already know.
  5. 05
    Consistent UI artButtons are distinct by shape, size and style for each level.
  6. 06
    PolishFeedback on everything: animation, gleams and response to touch.
  7. 07
    A tutorial that shows, not tellsSimple, clear and quick. It never feels like work.

3potential problems

  1. 01
    Room to growThe navigation is limited to five features. Adding a sixth will force a structural decision.
  2. 02
    Popups over popupsUsually a mistake. Here it is one level deep, a confirmation, and always returns to the previous screen, so it holds.
  3. 03
    Colour coding collisionThe level droplet uses the same colour as epic cards, which muddies what that colour means.
